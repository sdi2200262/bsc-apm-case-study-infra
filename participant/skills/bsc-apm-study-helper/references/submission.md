# Submission

After each session, the participant submits that session's zip and a short post-session questionnaire through a single submission form. The form is filled once per session, immediately after wrapping up; the URL is in step 5 of the participant guide's *Wrapping up* section. If the participant does not have the URL to hand, refer them to the study contact in `SKILL.md`.

## Submission checklist

- Session 1: form submitted with `P001_S1_{apm|speckit}.zip` attached.
- Session 2: form submitted with `P001_S2_{apm|speckit}.zip` attached, framework swapped.
